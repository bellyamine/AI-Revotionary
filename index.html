MODEL 01 — SIGNAL PROPAGATION, INPUT TO INFERENCE
§01

Trace of the revolution

1958

The perceptron

Frank Rosenblatt builds a machine that adjusts its own weights from example, the first working sketch of a network that learns rather than one that's told what to do.

2012

Deep learning breaks through

A deep network wins an image recognition contest by a wide margin, proving that stacking enough layers on enough data on enough compute changes what's possible, not just what's efficient.

2017

The transformer

A new architecture lets a model weigh every word against every other word at once. It becomes the shared circuit board underneath nearly every major model that follows.

2022–2023

The public switch flips

Conversational models leave the lab and reach hundreds of millions of people directly. For the first time, "using AI" becomes something anyone can do from a browser tab.

NOW

From answers to action

The frontier shifts from models that talk to systems that act: writing and running code, browsing, operating software, and working alongside people on real, multi-step tasks.
